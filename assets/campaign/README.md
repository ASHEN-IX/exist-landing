These PNG masters are AI-generated concept photography created for this landing
page. They replace low-resolution crops of a previous mockup. They are illustrative
campaign assets, not official EXIST product photographs.

Rebuild optimized WebP delivery images with `bash scripts/build-images.sh`.
Rebuild the four cinematic motion studies and their posters with
`bash scripts/build-media.sh all`. These are animated stills, not filmed footage.

The `-hq` delivery filenames bypass caches of the previous mockup imagery.
Keep the PNG masters outside `public` so visitors only download optimized assets.
